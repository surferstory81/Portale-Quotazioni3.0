## Rates by Engagement Type

### Time & Materials
- Standard rates apply
- Typically hourly or daily billing

### Fixed Price Projects
- 10-20% premium on standard rates to cover risk
- Milestone-based payments

### Dedicated Teams
- 5-10% discount for long-term engagements (>6 months)
- Monthly retainer model
